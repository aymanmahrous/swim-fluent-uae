# OFFICIAL_FACE_REFERENCE_LOCK

Status: `OWNER_APPROVED_NON_NEGOTIABLE_IDENTITY_RULE`

Approved by the owner on 2026-08-01.

## Official identity

Coach Ayman's approved face is the only official coach identity for Swim Fluent UAE and Relax Fix UAE.

Any design, image, video frame, advertising creative, website visual, social-media asset, poster, story, reel cover, brochure, or generated concept that shows the coach must use Coach Ayman's approved original face only.

## Mandatory identity lock

The following characteristics must remain unchanged:

- face shape
- eyes
- nose
- mouth and smile
- jawline
- hairline and hairstyle
- distinctive chin and beard pattern
- natural skin tone
- apparent age
- overall identity

## Prohibited actions

- No face swap.
- No regenerated or reconstructed face.
- No alternate male model representing the coach.
- No AI-created replacement head, body, pose, hair, beard, or clothing that changes identity.
- No cosmetic smoothing or retouching that changes facial features.
- No age change.
- No changes to eyes, nose, mouth, jawline, hairline, beard pattern, or skin tone.
- No redesign from scratch when an approved source, layout, or visual direction already exists.
- No use of a previous rejected or superseded face variant.

## Allowed operations

Only the following reversible operations are allowed on the approved original source:

- direct crop or reframing
- direct cutout and background removal
- careful cleanup of hair and edge masks
- minor exposure, white-balance, contrast, and color correction
- light sharpening that does not alter identity
- natural placement inside an approved layout

These operations must preserve the original face, head, body pose, and identity.

## Design continuity

New work must preserve:

- the approved Coach Ayman face
- the approved Swim Fluent UAE / Relax Fix UAE visual identity
- approved navy, turquoise, white, and gold brand direction where applicable
- existing approved source files and layouts whenever available
- exact version and source traceability

## Agent execution rule

Every design, content, image, video, Canva, marketing, website, and QA agent must treat this file as a non-negotiable source of truth.

Before creating or editing an asset that shows the coach, the agent must:

1. locate the approved original face source;
2. confirm it is Coach Ayman's locked reference;
3. reuse the approved design/source when available;
4. reject any output containing another face or altered identity;
5. record the source file or design reference used;
6. obtain human visual approval before publication.

Failure of the identity lock is a blocking QA defect. The asset must not be scheduled, published, deployed, or used in advertising.

## Release boundary

This identity approval does not by itself authorize publishing, scheduling, Production deployment, Ads, billing, spend, or external account writes. Existing release and approval gates remain in force.
